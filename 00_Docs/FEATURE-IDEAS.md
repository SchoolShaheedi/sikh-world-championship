# Feature Recommendations
Date: 2026-08-21
Ranked by (value to you) / (effort to build). Nothing here is committed — pick from it.

## TIER A — build early, high payoff, low cost

**1. Waitlist that auto-promotes.** Free events no-show 20-30%. When someone withdraws,
the next waitlister gets an automatic email. Costs an hour, saves your event from a
half-empty room.

**2. QR check-in on the day.** Each registrant gets a QR in their confirmation email.
Volunteer scans it, they're marked present. Beats a volunteer scrolling a 64-row spreadsheet
with a queue of teenagers in front of them. Also gives you a real no-show number.

**3. Live bracket page on a public URL.** Big screen in the hall shows it; parents at home
refresh it. Enormous perceived professionalism for very little work, and it's the single
best thing you can put on Instagram during the event.

**4. Auto-generated player cards.** A FIFA-style card for each entrant — name, gamertag,
photo, division. They post it themselves ("I'm in"). Free marketing, and kids love them.
This is the highest-leverage marketing feature on the list.

**5. Volunteer / helper sign-up.** Same form engine, different event type. You will need
12-20 volunteers and you do not want that in WhatsApp.

**6. Sponsor page + sponsor pack.** A public "our sponsors" page with logos is what you
actually sell to sponsors. Needed before you can ask anyone for money.

## TIER B — the platform, phase 2

**7. Player profiles.** Gamertag, division, region, games played, events attended, trophies
won. The spine everything else attaches to.

**8. LFG board + quick messages.** See CHAT-AND-SAFETY.md. This is the retention engine.

**9. Cross-event trophy cabinet.** A player who wins FIFA, enters chess, and places in
kabaddi has a profile that shows all three. This is what makes it a *championship
organisation* rather than a series of unrelated events. Strongly recommended — it's the
thing that makes people come back for event two.

**10. Chapters / regions.** Birmingham, Southall, Leicester, Slough, Toronto, Vancouver,
Delhi. Local organisers run local qualifiers, winners come to a national/world final.
This is how you get to "World" honestly — and how the thing scales without you running
every event personally.

**11. Global ranking / points.** Points for entering, more for placing, across all events
and all sports. Creates a reason to enter events you're not sure about.

## TIER C — bigger swings, later

**12. Online qualifiers feeding an in-person final.** Once online play is built, run
brackets remotely and fly/drive the top 8 to a live final. This is the actual route to
"World Sikh Championship" being literally true.

**13. Livestream + commentary.** Twitch/YouTube for the semis and final. Sikh kids seeing
their own community's tournament broadcast is genuinely powerful. Needs one person with
OBS and a capture card.

**14. Parent/guardian portal.** Guardians see their child's registrations, consents, and
chat settings in one place. Turns your biggest compliance burden into a trust feature.

**15. Seva / community layer.** Tie events to something beyond the game — a food bank
collection at the door, a charity total on the site, langar at every event. It's also,
practically, what unlocks gurdwara venues and community funding.

**16. Multi-language.** Gurmukhi/Punjabi toggle on the site. Matters a lot for parents
and for the diaspora outside the UK.

## THINGS TO DELIBERATELY NOT BUILD (yet)
- Native apps before there's a reason. The web app works on every phone today.
- Payments. Free entry avoids an entire category of problems. Keep it free as long as you can.
- Open DMs for under-16s. See CHAT-AND-SAFETY.md.
- A custom bracket engine for 64 players when a whiteboard works. Build the *public view*
  of the bracket; the admin side can be manual for event one.
- Leaderboards before there are multiple events to rank across.
