# 05_Partners_Sponsors

Sponsorship and partnership material. The public-facing page is `/sponsors`
(`03_App/web/src/app/sponsors/page.tsx`); this folder holds what sits behind it.

What belongs here: the sponsorship deck and tier/pricing sheet, partner agreements,
gurdwara and community-organisation contacts, in-kind offers (venue, PS5 consoles and
screens, langar, prizes), and sponsor logo files with their permitted-use terms.

## Note before approaching anyone

Two things should be true first: the domain and social handles secured
(`00_Docs/NEXT-STEPS.md`, "Do this week"), and the safeguarding paperwork in `04_Legal/`
underway. Sponsors and gurdwara committees will ask about child protection before they
ask about anything else, and "in progress" is a far better answer than "TBC".

> Signed agreements and anything with a sponsor's commercial terms in it should not go in
> a public repository. Keep them in shared storage and track the link here.
