## The short version

Play hard, be decent, remember whose name is on the event.

## What we expect

1. **Respect everyone** — opponents, volunteers, referees, the venue and its staff.
2. **Play fairly.** No cheating, no exploits, no colluding on results, no playing under
   someone else's name.
3. **Lose well and win well.** Shake hands. No gloating, no blaming, no controller-throwing.
4. **No abuse of any kind** — no racism, no casteism, no sexism, no homophobia, no
   comments about anyone's religion, appearance, disability or family. This includes
   "banter" that the person on the receiving end is not enjoying.
5. **Keep it clean.** This is a Sikh event with children present and langar being served.
   No alcohol, no drugs, no smoking or vaping on the premises, no foul language.
6. **Respect the space.** It is very likely a gurdwara or community hall — cover your head
   where asked, remove shoes where asked, and treat langar with respect.
7. **Look after younger players.** If you are 16 or over and a younger player is struggling,
   help them. The bracket matters less than that.
8. **Online, the same rules apply.** No asking another player for personal details, no
   trying to move a conversation onto another platform, and never contact an under-16 you
   met here outside the platform.

## Adults and children

If you are 18 or over: do not seek out one-to-one contact with a child you met through SWC,
online or in person. Not because we assume the worst, but because the boundary protects
everyone including you.

## If something goes wrong

Tell a volunteer or a safeguarding lead — they are identifiable on the day. Online, use
report on the post or profile, or the Support page. You can report anonymously. We aim to
respond within 24 hours.

## Consequences

Depending on what happened: a word, a warning, disqualification from the event, removal
from the venue, a ban from future events, or a referral to the police or social services.
Safeguarding matters are referred regardless of what the person or their family would prefer.

Decisions are made by `[the safeguarding lead / the organising team]`. Appeal to
`[EMAIL]`.
